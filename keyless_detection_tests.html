What the results show

The signal is real: shuffled labels, the unigram control and the unwatermarked-reference control all stay at chance. It comes from the key, not from style: watermarked texts agree with each other on context-specific word choices, and not with unwatermarked text in general. It peaks at 4 tokens of context, which matches SynthID's default n-gram length of 5.

Counting alone stalls around 0.55 to 0.56 because most 4-token contexts appear only once or twice, so the unwatermarked counts used as a baseline are mostly noise (only 9% of a test text's combinations were seen in training at h=4). Replacing that baseline with Gemma's own no-watermark probabilities gives an exact expected value for every context and is the main source of the gain, from 0.557 to 0.596 paired.

zw is the variant closest to a real deployment: it uses only watermarked texts plus the model, no unwatermarked twins. With near-certain positions removed it reaches 0.601 paired and 0.565 AUC.

Data: Google DeepMind SynthID-Text human_eval.jsonl, Gemma-7B-IT answers to ELI5 questions. 2841 of 3000 pairs used: pairs with fewer than 20 tokens dropped, and each pair truncated to the shorter answer's length so length cannot reveal the label. Tokenized with the Gemma-7B-IT tokenizer.

Evaluation: #1 to #6 use 5-fold CV split by question. #7 and #8 use no labels at all, and #9 and #10 compare each text against all other texts, never itself or its pair partner. #11 onward use leave-one-pair-out counts (a pair never sees itself or its partner); stacked models add 10-fold CV over pairs for the weights. Leave-one-pair-out uses more training data than 5-fold, worth about one point in #11 vs #3, so cross-setup comparisons should account for this. Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals on paired accuracy.

Model step: Gemma-7B-IT in 4-bit on a T4 GPU, top-40 next-token logits saved for every position. The question text is not in the file, so each answer was scored after a placeholder user turn ("Answer the question.") in Gemma's chat template. Temperature fitted on unwatermarked texts only: 1.25, with the chosen token in the top 40 for 98.5% of positions. Only the first occurrence of each context in a text is counted.

Settings such as h=4, the total aggregation and the 0.9 threshold were chosen after seeing results, so headline numbers are mildly optimistic. Differences under about one point are within noise.
